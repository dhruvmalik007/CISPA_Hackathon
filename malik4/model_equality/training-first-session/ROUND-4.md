Round 3 failed for two clear structural reasons: **(1) `r1` (1 sample per endpoint) at `T=1**` has huge sampling variance on a 200-word continuation, and **(2) transferring a hardcoded scalar threshold (`0.871066`)** from `ref-01..ref-09` (1 family + 4 singletons) to `ep-01..ep-33` (multiple families + within-family quantizations/LoRAs) collapsed 20 endpoints into `M1` (`MCC = 0.0`).

Below is a **16-method incremental research & testing pipeline** (grounded in published papers) that your coding agent can execute **sequentially and autonomously**—evaluating every method on **both** `ref-01..ref-09` (free) **and** your **Doubleword + Local GPU Proxy Zoo** before spending any of your remaining `87` live queries/endpoint on `ep-01..ep-33`.

### Phase 1: Black-Box Perturbation & Gradient-Surrogate Fingerprinting (Highest Signal-to-Budget)

These methods stop comparing raw responses directly (which system prompts and watermarks corrupt) and instead compare **how a model's output changes when the input is perturbed**—canceling out static system prompts and watermarks.

1. **SetPrint — Setwise Correspondence of Response Changes** (*Han et al., Nov 2026, `arXiv:2611.12950*`)
* **Mechanism:** Send a base prompt $q_0$ and $P$ slight perturbations $\{q_1, \dots, q_P\}$. Embed responses with `all-mpnet-base-v2`, compute response-change vectors $\Delta_p = \phi(y_p) - \phi(y_0)$, center them within each model's set, and score between-model correspondence across matching perturbations.
* **Why it beats Round 3:** System prompts and watermarks shift $y_0$ and $y_p$ in the *same* direction, so $\Delta_p$ cancels them out while preserving base-model and quantization/LoRA sensitivity.


2. **ZeroPrint — Black-Box Zeroth-Order Jacobian Estimation** (*Shao et al., WWW 2026, `arXiv:2510.06605*`)
* **Mechanism:** Construct semantically similar perturbed queries via targeted word substitution, embed both input differences ($\Delta X$) and output differences ($\Delta Y$), and fit a regularized linear regression $\hat{J} = \arg\min_J \Vert{}\Delta Y - J \Delta X\Vert{}_F^2$ to estimate each endpoint's local **Jacobian matrix** as its fingerprint.
* **Test:** Compare Frobenius / cosine similarity of $\hat{J}_i$ vs. $\hat{J}_j$ across endpoints.


3. **MET — Prompt-Conditioned Unbiased Two-Sample MMD** (*Gao et al., ICLR 2025, `arXiv:2410.20247*`)
* **Mechanism:** Never use `r1`. Collect $N \ge 5$ repeats per prompt, compute **unbiased** $\widehat{\text{MMD}}_u^2$ *within each prompt separately* using a position-wise **Hamming token kernel** (first 32–64 tokens), and aggregate p-values via permutation testing.


4. **Prefix-Tree / Token-Trie Branching Statistics** (*ICASSP 2026, `DOI:10.1109/ICASSP55912.2026.11461352*`)
* **Mechanism:** Build a token prefix tree (trie) from $N$ repeated completions of the same prompt. Compare endpoints by their **trie branching factor, shared-prefix depth distribution, and subtree KL divergence**.
* **Why it works:** Quantized vs. unquantized checkpoints (`fp16` vs. `int4`) branch at different token depths even when they share the same vocabulary.

### Phase 2: Disguise-Invariant Distributional & Stylometric Fingerprints

5. **LLMmap — Learned Trace Transformer** (*Pasquini et al., `arXiv:2407.15847*`)
* **Mechanism:** Embed `(prompt, response)` pairs, concatenate + project them, and pass the unordered set of traces through a lightweight positional-encoding-free self-attention encoder trained on your **Doubleword + Local GPU Proxy Zoo** (spanning 60+ system prompts, temperatures, watermarks, quantizations, and LoRAs).


6. **AgentProv — Policy & Behavioral Boundary Probes** (*Wang et al., ICLR 2026, `arXiv:2609.00052` — authored by the CISPA challenge organizers*)
* **Mechanism:** Use borderline instruction-following, tool-formatting, and refusal-boundary probes where **LoRA adapters** and **base families** diverge sharply in structure/refusal behavior while decoding watermarks have minimal impact.


7. **Surrogate Cross-Perplexity Fingerprinting (Local Open-Weight Bank)** (*inspired by LLMDet, Wu et al., 2023*)
* **Mechanism:** Take every response generated by `ep-01..ep-33` and score its **negative log-likelihood (perplexity)** locally on your GPUs under a bank of 6–8 open-weight models (e.g., `OLMo-2`, `Llama-3`, `Qwen-2.5`, `Mistral`, `Gemma-2`, plus `int4` variants).
* **Why it's massive:** Even if `ep-i` is watermarked or system-prompted, its text will have systematically lower perplexity under its own true base family than under other families—giving you a dense, continuous $K$-dimensional fingerprint vector per response with **zero extra API calls**.


8. **Watermark Detection & Pre-Filtering (KGW / SynthID Statistical Z-Score)** (*Kirchenbauer et al., ICML 2023*)
* **Mechanism:** Measure token-level lexical diversity, rare-token skew, and bigram entropy across endpoints to flag which endpoints carry a decoding watermark. Down-weight token-exact kernels on watermarked pairs and rely on **semantic embedding / SetPrint / cross-perplexity** features instead.


9. **Energy Distance & Sliced Wasserstein Distance on Dense Embeddings** (*Székely & Rizzo; Bonneel et al.*)
* **Mechanism:** Replace edit distance (which breaks under 1-word watermark shifts or system-prompt preambles) with **Energy Distance** or **Sliced Wasserstein Distance** over `all-mpnet-base-v2` / `BGE-large` sentence embeddings of $N \ge 5$ samples per prompt.


10. **Multi-Scale Stylometric & Structural Feature Vector** (*Writeprints / Authorship Attribution*)
* **Mechanism:** Extract watermark-invariant structural habits: Markdown header usage, bullet style (`-` vs. `*` vs. `1.`), punctuation ratios, discourse-marker frequencies (`"However,"`, `"In summary,"`), sentence-length variance, and code-formatting idioms (variable naming, docstring style).

### Phase 3: Active Prompt Optimization & Statistical Calibration

11. **TRAP — Targeted Random / High-Leverage Prompt Search** (*Gubri et al., 2024, `arXiv:2402.12991*`)
* **Mechanism:** Instead of hand-writing generic encyclopedia prompts (`ft01`), automatically search/evolve a pool of 100+ candidate prompts on your **Doubleword + Local GPU Proxy Zoo** to find the exact 6–8 prompts that maximize **within-family quantization/LoRA separation** while minimizing **same-model disguise variance**.


12. **RUT-Inspired Rank-Based Uniformity / Relative-Rank Tests** (*Zhu et al., ICLR 2026, `arXiv:2506.06975*`)
* **Mechanism:** While full RUT uses a local authentic model, you can apply its **rank-based non-parametric uniformity principle** across your **local proxy bank** (Method 7) to test whether two endpoints' token-rank distributions under a reference tokenizer/LM are statistically indistinguishable.


13. **Supervised Contrastive (`SupCon`) + Hard-Negative Triplet Verifier** (*Khosla et al., NeurIPS 2020*)
* **Mechanism:** Train a `DeBERTa-v3-large` / `ModernBERT` verifier on your **Doubleword + Local GPU Proxy Zoo** where positives = same checkpoint under different system prompts/watermarks/temps, hard negatives = same family with different quantization/LoRA, and easy negatives = different families.

### Phase 4: Fixing the Clustering & Threshold-Transfer Failure (Crucial for `ep-01..ep-33`)

Even a great distance matrix will score `0.0` if you transfer a hardcoded scalar threshold from `n=9` to `n=33`. Add these three clustering upgrades to your agent's pipeline:

14. **Spectral Clustering + Eigengap Heuristic (Scale-Free Cluster Count)** (*von Luxburg, 2007*)
* **Mechanism:** Compute the normalized graph Laplacian of the $33 \times 33$ similarity matrix and pick the number of `family` clusters $K_{\text{fam}}$ from the **largest eigengap** ($\lambda_{k+1} - \lambda_k$) rather than a hardcoded threshold from `ref-*`.


15. **Two-Stage Hierarchical Consensus Clustering (Ensemble of Methods 1–13)**
* **Mechanism:** Never rely on 1 prompt or 1 distance metric. Standardize (z-score) the upper triangle of the distance matrices from your top 4 orthogonal methods (e.g., `SetPrint` + `Surrogate Cross-Perplexity` + `Prompt-Stratified Embedding Energy Distance` + `SupCon Verifier`), average them into a **consensus distance matrix**, and run average-linkage clustering with **Silhouette / Calinski-Harabasz** selection inside each family.


16. **Adaptive Active-Query Refinement (Spend Remaining Budget Only on Boundary Pairs)**
* **Mechanism:** Spend only **40–48 queries/endpoint** (`6–8` proxy-validated prompts × `6–8` repeats) on `ep-01..ep-33` first. Identify the **uncertain pairs** sitting near the within-family `model` decision boundary, and spend your final **35–40 queries/endpoint** *exclusively* on high-discriminative probes (like `SetPrint` perturbations or `TRAP` boundary prompts) to resolve those exact pairs.
